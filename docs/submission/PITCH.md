# PixelPets — pitch outline (6–8 slides)

Talking points for a ~3 minute live pitch. Demo video covers the click path separately.

---

## Slide 1 — Hook (20s)

- **Title:** PixelPets — *A tiny companion. A world of stories. Collect the journey.*
- **Line:** What if your pixel pet left the house for you — and came back with a postcard you could keep, gift, and prove?
- **Audience:** cozy / pixel collectors + Solana wallet users who want something to *do* with a cute asset.
- **Not:** another mint → flip → ghost project.

---

## Slide 2 — Player loop (30s)

1. **Adopt** free (Moss / Puddle / Wisp) — no wallet required.
2. **Care** on soft timers; pets never die from neglect.
3. **Travel** — pick place + packing; demo trips are labelled accelerated.
4. **Return** — story postcard + keepsake chance → **Album**.
5. **Optional chain** — claim Core asset, save journey receipt, gift to a friend.

*Emotion order:* prepare → wait → surprise → show → share.

---

## Slide 3 — Uniqueness (25s)

| vs | We take | We leave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travel Frog | Offline hope + souvenirs | Cash gambling wrapper |
| Typical NFT pet | Ownership + gift on Solana | Daily feeds on-chain, token emissions |
| Axie-style grind | Soft bond / forms | Speculative breeding day-one |

- Keepsakes = **collection records**, not eight NFT types.
- Relay/legacy story compressed into **gift with public journey card**, not forced selling of the pet you love.

---

## Slide 4 — Solana usage (30s)

- **Metaplex Core** collection + 3 limited editions on **Devnet**.
- **Claim** co-signed; **PetRecord** stores checkpoint hashes.
- **Journey receipt (checkpoint v2)** — settled trip result hashed; public proof id independently verified (10 checks).
- **Gift** = owner-signed Core transfer; recipient continues care after sync.
- Wallet Standard bridge with human-readable review (network, asset, fee).

## Slide 6 — Traction plan (20s)

- Hackathon: strangers complete adopt → first trip without coaching.
- Next: 20–30 cozy players, measure second trip + wallet opt-in (not vanity mints).
- Kill criteria: if everyone only asks “wen token / wen floor,” fix the toy — don’t raise emissions.

---

## Slide 7 — Business model without a token (25s)

- **Free** care + supplies (non-redeemable).
- **Later paid:** previewable cosmetics, room frames, theme route packs (no payout boost).
- **Later peer:** trade duplicate keepsakes / extra companions; small protocol fee only if atomic settlement exists.
- **No** project token until (if ever) unpaid retention + real cosmetic buyers exist — see product doc §8.

Sensitivity: 1k MAU × 4% pay × $5 ≈ $200/mo merchandise — fees alone don’t fund a studio. Be honest.
